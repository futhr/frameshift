# Candidate Bill of Materials

**Research only — not a purchase list.**

No component in this file is approved until its exact revision, interface, electrical requirements, dimensions, availability and price are rechecked.

## Photo Frame

- ~27-inch raw matte IPS, preferably QHD/eDP.
- Compatible display controller if the panel cannot be driven directly.
- Minimal Wi-Fi-capable frame agent.
- Thin power solution.
- Wood frame, backing and passe-partout.

## Paper Frame

### Prototype
- Small color e-paper/Spectra-class development display (~13.3-inch candidate).
- Compatible controller, ideally ESP32-S3-class.
- Local storage if not integrated.
- Power appropriate to refresh behavior.

### Future A2
- ~28.5-inch/A2 reflective color panel candidate.
- Vendor-compatible waveform/controller hardware.

## Pixel Frame

- Six P3-class 64x64 HUB75 modules (~192 mm square candidate).
- ESP32-S3 HUB75 controller candidate.
- Appropriately sized low-profile PSU.
- Distribution wiring/fusing as required.
- Structural carrier and diffuser/surface experiments if desired.

## Rule

URLs, prices and exact SKUs belong in dated sourcing research once independently verified. Architecture must not silently depend on an unvalidated marketplace listing.
