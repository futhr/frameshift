# Display Technology Research

## Matte IPS

Best current path for an inexpensive large photographic prototype. Commodity supply, excellent color/resolution and easy sourcing are advantages. Emissive appearance, continuous power and backlight depth distinguish it from paper.

A matte surface, restrained brightness, color calibration, passe-partout and physical framing should be treated as part of the experiment.

## Reflective e-paper

Best conceptual match for a digital print. Advantages include reflective appearance, image retention and very thin panel surfaces. Current large-format color pricing is the primary obstacle.

The strategy is therefore to validate the software/rendering stack on a small premium panel before considering A2 hardware.

## HUB75 RGB matrix

Not a cheaper IPS substitute. It is a separate artistic medium. Coarse pixels, light emission and limited resolution become deliberate features when the host transforms source photography for the medium.

The hidden challenge is power/thermal design rather than controller compute.

## Shared conclusion

Do not force these technologies behind identical electrical hardware. Standardize the protocol, capability model, asset semantics and host experience instead.
